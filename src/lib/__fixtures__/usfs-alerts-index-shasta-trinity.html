  • Critical

    Critical danger may be present. Please take all precautions and recommendations to avoid harm.

  • Fire Restriction

    The USFS initiates restrictions or temporary public land closures to reduce the risk of wildfires and protect the public.

  • Caution

    A notification or warning that may impede normal planned recreational activities. Caution warnings indicate possible or impending danger or may otherwise result in an unpleasant or unsafe situation while recreating on the forest.

  • Information

    While generally not dangerous in nature, informational alerts identify situations that could affect visitor plans or be of importance or concern or to the general public.
